Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the ConverterDoc app for mobile devices, together with any related services operated by Nayaka Digital (collectively, the "Application"). Nayaka Digital is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".


Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information when you download and use it. This information may include:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol address
  • The pages, screens, or features of the Application that you visit, the time and date of your visit, and the time spent on those features
  • The time spent on the Application
  • Your mobile operating system, device model, and performance/crash metrics
  • Account Information: When you sign in using Google Authentication, the Service Provider receives basic profile information (such as your account ID and email address) to manage your account status, coin balance, and purchase entitlements. If you use Guest or Demo mode, no personal authentication credentials are collected.
  • Document and Media Processing: To provide conversion services, the Application processes user-selected files (PDF, images, TXT, HTML, CSV), physical scans captured via camera OCR, and imported web URLs. All files, document contents, and camera captures are processed solely for the conversion workflow to generate Markdown (.md) documents and are not sold, inspected, or utilized for profiling.

Device Permissions

The Application requests explicit permissions strictly needed to perform its core functions:

  • Camera: Used exclusively when capturing physical documents, receipts, or notes for instant OCR text conversion.
  • Storage / Media: Used to access documents or images selected by you for conversion and to save exported Markdown (.md) files to your device.

Account and Data Deletion

ConverterDoc gives users full transparency and control over their personal data:

  • In-App Account Deletion: Users can permanently delete their account and associated data directly within the Application by navigating to Settings > Delete Account. Initiating this action immediately wipes local app storage and unlinks your authentication record.
  • Web Deletion Request: If you have uninstalled the Application or cannot access your device, you can submit a deletion request without reinstalling the app. Please send an email to the Service Provider at digitalnayaka@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request", specifying the Google account email address associated with your ConverterDoc account.
  • Data Retention & Permanent Wipe: Upon receiving an in-app or written account deletion request, the Service Provider permanently wipes your account profile, linked email address, cloud-synced coin ledger, and transaction identifiers within 30 days. No user document contents are stored on remote servers; all conversions occur locally on your device or via temporary memory processing.

Data Retention Policy

The Service Provider retains personal data based on its necessity for the stated purposes:

  • User Provided Data: Retained for the duration of your use of the Application plus 12 months thereafter, unless longer retention is required by law
  • Automatically Collected Data: Retained for up to 24 months from collection, unless longer retention is required for legal compliance
  • Aggregated and Anonymized Data: Retained indefinitely as it no longer identifies you
  • Data required for legal compliance: Retained as long as required by applicable law

Locally stored conversion history can be deleted directly within the Application at any time. If you want the Service Provider to delete User Provided Data submitted through the Application, please contact them at digitalnayaka@gmail.com.
