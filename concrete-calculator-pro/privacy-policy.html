Privacy Policy for Concrete Calculator

This Privacy Policy applies to the Concrete Calculator mobile application (collectively, the "Application") operated by Nayaka Digital (referred to as the "Service Provider", "we", "us", or "our").

1. Information Collection and Use

The Application is designed with user privacy in mind. Most features, calculations, and profile data operate locally on your device.

Automatically Collected Information:

When you download and use the Application, third-party services and analytics tools may collect non-personal device diagnostics, including:

  • Device Internet Protocol (IP) address
  • Mobile operating system version, device model, and performance/crash metrics
  • App usage statistics (e.g., feature interactions, session durations)

Contractor & Quote Profile Data:

  • Business Profile Details: Information entered in the settings (such as Business/Contractor Name, Contact Number, and Email/Website) is stored locally on your device to personalize generated PDF quotes and estimates. This information is not transmitted to our remote servers.
  • Project Calculation Inputs: Dimensions, bag rates, ready-mix truck costs, and custom added project items are processed locally on your device for real-time calculations and PDF export.

2. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

The Application offers optional PRO subscription plans ($1.99/monthly, $17.99/yearly, and $49.99 lifetime access) processed exclusively via the Google Play In-App Billing system.

The Service Provider does not collect, store, or receive your credit card, debit card, or financial account details. All transaction authentication and payment processing are handled securely by Google Play.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertisements

The Application utilizes third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device or serve advertisements (Banner, Interstitial, and Rewarded Ads). You can review the privacy policies of our third-party service providers below:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play In-App Billing

4. Device Permissions

The Application may request permissions strictly necessary to fulfill requested features:

  • Storage / Media Access: Required to save exported PDF quotes, project summaries, and material cost reports to your device's local file storage.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Policy

Because your contractor profile details, calculation history, and custom material items are stored locally on your device, you have complete control over your data:

  • In-App Data Reset: You can clear your contractor profile or custom inputs at any time directly within the application settings.
  • App Uninstallation: Uninstalling the Application permanently removes all locally stored data, preferences, and custom calculation entries.
  • Written Data Deletion Requests: To request assistance or inquire about any data processed by third-party SDKs, send an email to digitalnayaka@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request - Concrete Calculator". Requests will be fulfilled within 30 days.

6. California Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

California residents have the right to request access to or deletion of their personal information, and the right to opt out of any sale or sharing of personal information. The Service Provider does not sell personal information. To exercise your California privacy rights, contact us at digitalnayaka@gmail.com.

7. European Economic Area (GDPR) & International Data Transfers

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, your data is processed in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Where third-party service providers transfer data internationally, standard contractual clauses and recognized transfer mechanisms are applied.

8. Children’s Privacy

The Application is not intended for children under 13 years of age (or 16 in certain jurisdictions). We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal data from children. If we discover that a child has provided us with personal information, we will immediately take steps to delete it. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us at digitalnayaka@gmail.com.

9. Security

We value your trust in providing us your information. We maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect information processed by the Application. However, please remember that no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the updated Privacy Policy on this page with a revised effective date.
